What is 600 N MILE?
In fact, it is the distance between the hometown of the host and the hostess (the difference between Taiwan and Xiamen is 160 nautical miles, but in order to make the name more attractive and deep, the art is processed into 600 nautical miles), and then we can imagine many scenes: 600 nautical miles Food, love in 600 nautical miles, wind and rain in 600 nautical miles.
